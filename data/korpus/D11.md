# Ground Handling SLA Annex

## 1. Purpose

This annex sets out the service levels that ground handling providers must meet at Gökçe Airport (XXXX). The values in this document are fictional and must not be used in real operations.

## 2. Turnaround service levels

For a narrow-body aircraft, the handling provider must complete passenger disembarkation within 12 minutes of the aircraft chocks being placed. Baggage delivery to the first belt must begin within 15 minutes of on-block time. Catering and cleaning must be finished at least 10 minutes before the scheduled boarding start.

## 3. Equipment availability

Ground power, pre-conditioned air and a passenger stair or boarding bridge must be available at the stand at least 5 minutes before the scheduled arrival time. If equipment is not ready, the provider must inform the AOCC shift supervisor immediately.

## 4. Reporting

The provider submits a monthly performance report listing every turnaround that missed its service level, together with the reason for the delay. The airport may audit these reports at any time.
