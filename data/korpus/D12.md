# Ground Handling Agreement Summary

## 1. Overview

This summary describes the main commercial terms of the ground handling agreement between the airport and its handling providers. It is a fictional training document; the amounts are not real.

## 2. Service levels and penalties

The table below lists the key service levels in the agreement and the penalty that applies when a provider misses the target.

| Service | Target | Measurement | Penalty per missed turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passenger disembarkation | Within 12 minutes of chocks on | Per turnaround | 150 credits |
| First bag on belt | Within 15 minutes of on-block | Per turnaround | 200 credits |
| Cleaning and catering | Complete 10 minutes before boarding | Per turnaround | 100 credits |
| Equipment ready at stand | 5 minutes before arrival | Per turnaround | 120 credits |
| Monthly report submission | By the 5th of the next month | Per month | 500 credits |

## 3. Review and termination

Service levels are reviewed every year. If a provider misses the first-bag target in more than 10 percent of turnarounds in any three consecutive months, the airport may start a formal review of the agreement.
